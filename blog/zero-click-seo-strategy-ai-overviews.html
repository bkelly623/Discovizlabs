Zero-click SEO strategy

Optimize for AI Overviews and answer engines without looking like a bot.

When AI answers the query directly, rankings and clicks stop being the whole story.

Your new job is to show up inside the answer.

The KPI: Share of Answer

Across a fixed prompt set, measure how often the AI mentions or cites you. Then iterate weekly.

That’s how you avoid guessing. That’s how you compound.

Three levers that move AI Overviews

  1. Answer-ready pages: headings, tight sections, FAQ schema, one clear claim per page.
  2. Entity clarity: consistent business description, same language everywhere, no semantic drift.
  3. Citation graph: credible third-party sources repeating your phrase.

What not to do

  • Don’t spam generic keywords or hashtag blocks (it reads automated and harms trust).
  • Don’t publish 20 thin posts that cannibalize each other.
  • Don’t treat AI visibility like vibes—measure it.
