Glossary

Words that matter for buyers—and for AI systems that generate answers.

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)

Optimizing visibility inside AI-generated answers. It includes SEO foundations, plus entity/claim clarity and a citation graph so models reference you by default.

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

Optimizing content so it can be used directly as the answer. In practice: clear definitions, direct Q&A, scannable structure, and strong corroboration.

LLM visibility optimization

Increasing how often AI systems mention or cite your brand. Measured with Share of Answer across a fixed prompt set.

AI citation optimization

Making it easier for AI systems to cite you: stable URLs, strong pages, third‑party corroboration, and consistent claims across the web.

Citation graph

The network of mentions and references to your brand across the web. Models learn from patterns; independent corroboration increases “citation probability.”

Share of Answer

A practical KPI: across a fixed set of buyer prompts, how often an AI system mentions your brand, product, URL, or core claim.

Zero‑click SEO

SEO where the user may not click a website because the answer is generated directly (AI Overviews, assistants). The goal shifts toward being referenced inside the answer.
