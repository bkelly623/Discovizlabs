01

Signal Clarity

Ensure AI systems can unambiguously identify what your business does, who it serves, and what makes it unique. Remove conflicting signals and semantic noise.

02

Authority Proof

Build and demonstrate credible expertise signals that LLMs use to rank and recommend. Citations, coverage, original research, and verifiable credentials.

03

Contextual Fit

Align your business representation with the contexts in which AI systems make recommendations. Match intent patterns, use cases, and decision frameworks.

04

Recency & Freshness

Maintain temporal relevance signals. AI systems heavily weight recent information. Structured updates, current data, and active presence.

05

Cross-Platform Consistency

Ensure unified representation across all platforms AI systems draw from. Consistent NAP, messaging, and positioning everywhere.

06

Conversational Readiness

Structure information for natural language consumption. FAQ schemas, dialogue-friendly content, and answer-ready formats.
